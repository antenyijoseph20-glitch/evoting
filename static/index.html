Step 1 — Identity

Enter the NIN and VIN on the voter register. A polling officer must register you before you can vote.

Exactly 11 digits.

Step 2 — One-time code

A six-digit code was sent to your registered phone.

The code expires after 5 minutes. Three incorrect attempts end the session.

Step 3 — Face check

Look straight at the camera in even lighting. Only one face should be visible.

Development builds only check that exactly one face is present. Production requires a vendor-backed matcher with liveness; the app refuses to start without one.

Step 4 — Cast ballot

Choose the election and party. You get one vote per election and it cannot be changed.

Only elections currently open are listed.

Vote recorded

Keep this receipt. It proves your ballot is in the public ledger without identifying you.
